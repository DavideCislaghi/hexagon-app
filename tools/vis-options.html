Visualiser Options

5 new modes beyond Bars & Waveform

Option 1 — Radial Burst
Spikes radiate from a central circle. Pulses with beat energy. Feels like a vinyl record or radar.
Option 2 — Particles
Dots drift upward, spawning faster on the beat. Speed and count driven by BPM.
Option 3 — Oscilloscope Ring
A circle that deforms into a wobbly shape. Dark, hypnotic. Morphs continuously.
Option 4 — DNA / Lissajous
Two sine waves interweave as a scrolling ribbon. BPM sets the frequency ratio. Elegant, technical.
Option 5 — Grid Pulse
A dot matrix where cells light up based on beat phase. Like a drum machine sequencer coming alive.
