HELP & GUIDE
How to use the app
Getting Started
What is Hexagon?
Hexagon is a personal workout planner. It holds a read-only exercise library, generates sessions based on your preferences, tracks your workout history over time, and plays your music while you train.
Navigation
Tap the ☰ menu (top-left) to open the drawer. Pages are organised into three groups: Workout (Session, Timers, History, Stats), Library (Exercises, Muscles, Equipment, Tags), and Music. Tap the header logo to return Home at any time.
First steps
Browse the Library to get familiar with the exercises. Then go to Workout › Session — or tap the big logo on the Home page — to generate your first session and start training.
Library
Read-only exercise library
The library is a curated, fixed set of exercises. You can browse and filter it in every way, but exercises cannot be added or deleted — this keeps the data clean and consistent for session generation. What you can always edit per exercise is the weight, sets, and reps.
Exercises
The full exercise list with search and sort. Tap any card to see its full description, tags, and equipment. From the card you can set the weight, working sets, and working reps for that exercise — these values are used directly when it appears in a session. If an exercise has no sets or reps set, the app falls back to the defaults in Settings › Session › Sets & Reps.
Muscles
Browse by muscle group. Each group expands into an accordion showing all its exercises. Tap an exercise card to view full details.
Equipment
Browse exercises by equipment type — great for planning around what gear you have available. In Settings you can exclude equipment types you don't have access to, so they're never picked in generated sessions.
Tags
Tags cover sub-muscles and movement types. Tap any tag row to see all exercises that share it — useful for targeting a specific movement pattern or secondary muscle.
Library updates
When a new version of the exercise library is available, an update badge appears on the Settings icon. Inside Settings › Data › Library, a banner lets you download the update with one tap. Your workout history and settings are never affected.
Sessions
Generate a session
Tap Generate Session and the app will automatically pick muscle groups and exercises based on your Settings (groups per session, exercises per group, sets & reps).
Re-rolling
Not happy with the selection? Tap Re-roll to generate a fresh set. Lock any exercises you want to keep before re-rolling — they'll stay in place while the rest are replaced.
Build a session manually
Tap Build Session for full manual control. A 4-step flow guides you through: (1) session settings — equipment, max time, rest duration, alert countdown; (2) pick your muscle groups; (3) choose exercises for each group; (4) preview and accept. Built sessions are colour-coded differently in History so you can tell them apart from generated ones.
Temporarily exclude muscles
In Settings › Session, you can temporarily exclude one or more muscle groups. Excluded muscles are skipped by the session generator until you re-enable them — useful when a muscle is sore or injured without permanently changing your setup.
Tracking sets
During a session, tap the ring circles next to each exercise to mark sets as completed. The session finishes automatically once all sets are checked off.
Rating a session
When a session completes, you'll be prompted to rate your perceived effort. There are 5 levels: Easy, Light, Moderate, Hard, and Max. This is optional — tap Skip if you'd rather not. The rating is saved with the session and visible in History.
Session variety & Session Mode
Hexagon tracks how often each exercise has been picked and favours less-used ones over time. You can also control this via Session Mode in Settings › Session › Favourites: Random ignores favourites entirely, Favour Favourites pushes them to the front of the pool, and Favourites Only restricts picks to your starred exercises only.
Timers
Session timer
Start the session timer before you begin. It tracks your total workout duration and must be running before other timer features become available.
Rest timer
After completing a set, the rest timer counts down your configured rest period. It only activates while the session timer is running. You can also trigger it manually from the Timers page.
On-the-fly rest duration
A row of duration pills is shown below the rest timer — both on the Timers page and inside the active session overlay. Tap any pill to switch to that rest length immediately. This is a temporary override for the current session only; it does not change your default rest duration in Settings.
Timers page
Access Workout › Timers for standalone timer controls — useful for warm-ups, cooldowns, or any timed activity outside a tracked session. Both the Session Timer and Rest Timer are shown as collapsible accordion sections; tap a header to expand or collapse it. The rest timer here has its own Stop button.
Music
Music page
Find all your workout tracks under Music in the drawer. Tracks are loaded from the library and can be searched, sorted by name, BPM, vibe, or duration, and filtered by vibe tag.
Playing a track
Tap any track card to start playing it. The persistent mini-player appears at the bottom of the screen across all pages. Tap the mini-player to expand it.
Session queue
Tap the queue icon on any track card to add it to your session queue. The queue plays in order when you start it from the player. Use Add all to queue to queue everything currently visible in one tap, or Clear queue to start fresh. The queue persists across page changes and app restarts.
Locked In
Tap the lock icon on a track card to mark it as Locked In — your go-to tracks. Use the Locked In filter pill above the track list to show only your favourites. Locked In status persists across sessions.
Full / Compact view
Toggle between Full (tile grid with album art and canvas visualiser) and Compact (row list) using the view toggle above the track list. Full view is the default.
Music Player button
A Music Player button appears on the Session timer card and the Timers page. Tap it to open the expanded player without navigating away — so you can control playback while watching the timer.
History & Stats
Workout history
Every completed session is automatically logged with date, duration, effort rating, muscle groups, exercises, and sets & reps. Access it under Workout › History.
Reviewing & rerunning a session
Tap any entry in History to expand its full details — exercises performed, sets completed, and total duration. You can also rerun it exactly as it was, loading the same muscle groups, exercises, and sets & reps into a fresh session.
Stats
The Stats page aggregates your full history — total sessions, total time trained, most-used muscles, most-picked exercises, and more.
Settings
Session tab
Controls workout generation: how many muscle groups and exercises appear per session, default sets & reps (used as fallback when an exercise has no individual values set), and your preferred sort order for the exercise library. You can also exclude equipment types you don't have access to, temporarily exclude muscle groups from the generator, and configure Session Mode to control how favourited exercises influence picks.
Timers tab
Set your default session length, rest timer duration, and alert countdown (the number of seconds before the rest timer ends when the urgent ticking begins). You can also choose the alert sound and toggle vibration.
Appearance tab
Toggle dark/light mode, choose from 15 accent colours (with separate defaults per mode), set the card border style, and adjust other visual preferences.
Data tab — Offline mode
Enable Offline Mode to download the full exercise library (including images) to your device. Once downloaded, the app works completely without an internet connection. Disable it at any time to revert to online mode.
Data tab — Export & Import
Export downloads your history and settings as a .json file. Import restores from a backup — this is how you move data between devices: export on the old device, import on the new one. Warning: importing replaces all existing data completely. There is no merge — the backup becomes the new state of the app.
Data tab — Clear session history
Deletes all past sessions and pick-count data, but keeps your settings and preferences intact. This cannot be undone — export first if you want a backup.
